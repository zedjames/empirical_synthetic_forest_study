# Prospective Health under Declared Specifications: An Auditable Empirical-Synthetic Forest Measurement Study

## Abstract

Health as organizational realization and adequate prospective capacity requires
a measurement model connecting observations to lawful continuations. We examine
that connection in a Harvard Forest cohort prototype through three questions:
measurement identification and calibration, decision reliability, and
query-relative representation. A general subset argument simplifies the reserve
query without changing finite-bank Health. Independent implementation audits
detect a reached Gamma shape-floor moment defect and an approximation from
pooling unequal observation exposures. Separate original and revised generators
preserve reproducible comparisons, including all adverse and unresolved cases.
Four oracle interfaces reveal strong state–parameter interactions rather than
an additive error partition. Fixed earlier-fit adult hazards are evaluated
against independent later tagged-stem observations; hemlock mortality is
underpredicted even under its full shared-hazard predictive distribution.
Terminal path attribution localizes the large reconstruction contrast entirely
to juvenile-support failure. Seedling observations provide descriptive dated transitions but
do not identify the prototype's whole-plot juvenile target. Exact structural
projection, stochastic dynamics, biological entry identification and numerical
inference remain distinct. The result is an auditable measurement study under
declared specifications, not a validated operational assessment of full forest
Health.

## Introduction

An organization may currently realize its defining functions yet lack adequate
capacity to continue under a given context. Conversely, a favorable modeled
future does not establish that its organization currently exists. A prospective
Health query therefore asks both questions, relative to a declared organization,
response space, horizon and requirement. Bringing that definition to
observations requires more than computing a scalar: the observation model,
candidate laws, representation and decision inference must support the query.

The forest prototype makes those interfaces explicit using tagged census
observations, cohort dynamics and a restricted organizational realization based
on basal area and juvenile abundance. We distinguish three questions. First,
are the reconstructed state and response laws empirically identified and
calibrated? Second, can the implemented estimator discriminate specified Health
decisions near their boundaries? Third, does a chosen representation preserve
the information required by those decisions? A successful executable
specification alone does not answer any of these empirical questions.

## Results: success criteria

Specification-execution success means that the declared mathematical objects
are correctly constructed, algorithms follow their declared laws, probability
mass is accounted for and results are reproducible. Empirical measurement
success additionally requires sufficiently identified latent quantities,
calibrated predictive laws, a representation sufficient for the licensed query
and uncertainty small enough for a defensible decision. We evaluate these
separately; the results below establish the former without asserting the latter
for full operational forest Health.

## Results: identification and calibration

The reserve-qualified viable event is contained in viability. For every
nonnegative unrenormalized measure, its capacity alone therefore determines the
two-part reserve adequacy inequality at a common threshold. Independent count
reconstruction preserves every finite decision while a single pointwise Wilson
interval removes redundant confidence constraints. Historical statuses remain
separately addressable. Exact finite success, including all-success samples,
does not establish kernel probability one.

The original Gamma shape floor was reached in 12 of 131,072 primary
ancestral-cell/scenario laws, across three parameter identities. Those branches
materially change actual moments. Mean-preserving scale corrects the law,
including explicit deterministic zero cases. The complete primary replay and
all reached-parameter high-precision panels preserve their observed finite
capacity/Health answers. That outcome does not make the original law correct,
nor establish identical continuous kernels or stability at every threshold.

The exposure audit retains 98,018 dated component records. Conditional survivor
counts follow a Poisson-binomial law under unequal durations; a mean-duration
Binomial is an approximation. Capturing the same nuisance draws isolates the
dated-component change from unrelated reconstruction RNG consumption. In the
primary ensemble the controlled mean count difference is 26.34375 stems, with
maximum absolute difference 471. Small mean shifts do not imply small decision
effects. Complete correction surfaces retain all local extrema and upper
quantiles alongside ensemble changes.

Independent later adult validation uses 21,578 eligible baseline-survivor stems.
For the 2024 primary cumulative window, 21,576 stems are scored: 1,477 secure
observed deaths versus 1,050.285 predicted deaths. Hemlock accounts for 685
observed deaths versus 280.348 predicted among 7,557 stems; its mortality gap is
0.053547. The other-stem gap is 0.001574. The fixed shared-cell-hazard
predictive law yields a hemlock median of 279 deaths and central 95% interval
220–347, with P(D ≥ 685) = 4.6308 × 10⁻²¹. Nonhemlock has mean
769.938, median 768 and interval 654–896 against 792 observed deaths;
its upper-tail probability is 0.353712. All adults have mean 1,050.285,
median 1,049 and interval 918–1,192 against 1,477 observed deaths;
the upper-tail probability is 3.2045 × 10⁻⁸.

Predictive variance separates conditional mortality-event variation from
shared-cell-hazard uncertainty. Those components are respectively 266.318 and
782.132 deaths² for hemlock, 693.360 and 3,121.328 for nonhemlock, and
959.678 and 3,903.460 for all adults. Shared hazards contribute 74.60%,
81.82% and 80.27% of total predictive variance. Thus underprediction cannot be
explained by omitting the model's own shared-hazard uncertainty. Independent
positive-probability convolutions reproduce the predictive law and rare tails;
262,144 explicit individual-event replicates and a separate Binomial sampler
agree with its central distribution. Zero hemlock exceedances in the simulations
are not interpreted as a zero probability. All annual windows, ambiguous-status
sensitivities and predeclared strata remain visible. No later outcomes refit the
model. Independent raw-source reconstruction reproduces every prediction and
score; checked bootstrap aggregation reproduces every interval endpoint.

Seedling evidence yields 8,840 dated descriptive survival transitions. Its
below-1-cm subplot support does not identify the whole-plot 1–10-cm juvenile
target. First observation is not germination; the graduation-coded identity is
already graduated at its first visit. The 6,992 later-only candidate-stem audit
likewise does not distinguish biological threshold crossing from earlier
missed eligible stems, new stems, identity change or frame change. Diagnostic
entry weights are not calibrated posteriors.

## Results: decision reliability

All original boundary and factorial worlds are retained. Original and revised
predictions are scored against each separately identified reference, rather
than replacing truth when the generator changes. Corrected reference precision
is reported explicitly; the original maximum reference remains distinct from
lower-precision revised banks. Synthetic observation packets lack individual
dates, so that correction is inapplicable to their estimator.

For nominal present cases within 0.1 of the boundary, the observation-only
estimator's original balanced accuracy is 0.5630 for continuation and 0.5549
for reserve. True state alone gives 0.6513 and 0.5890. True parameters alone
give 0.5147 and 0.5284; both give 1.0 on the certified cases. These are
constructed-design scores with explicitly unresolved references, not population
validation. State/parameter interactions are strong. The reserve parameter-only
mass-loss gain is negative, with a paired design-conditional interval excluding
zero. No additive nonnegative error allocation is imposed. Even both oracles
retain predictive-law misspecification and finite future Monte Carlo.

The largest local primary capacity difference remains near 0.956 at 4,096
paths in the targeted adverse case, versus 0.984375 at the original 64 paths.
It is not explained by the coarse original sampling noise. In the exact
state 205, parameter replicate 1, D0, one-year replay, pooled exposure has
43 viable paths and 4,053 failures among 4,096 candidates; individual exposure
has 3,959 viable paths and 137 failures. Every failure is terminal juvenile
support alone. Neither bank contains model-unlawful, basal-area-only or joint
failures. Both begin with present realization. The juvenile threshold is
65,661.6 stems; pooled and individual reconstructed origins have 66,600 and
66,900 juvenile stems respectively. The basal-area threshold is 739.312,
well below both origins and every terminal sampled basal area. This contrast
therefore comes from the juvenile continuation predicate under changed
reconstruction, not basal-area loss or discarded unlawful candidates. Raw
path flags retain all overlapping causes even though joint failures are zero
in this selected bank. Shared random stream identities do not imply a
pathwise causal matching when changing the reconstructed state changes random
sampling consumption. This is a threshold-sensitive local consequence, not
evidence of a uniform capacity change across states. Complete missingness and diagnostic
latent-fate/entry surfaces expose signed margins and tipping thresholds without
assigning ecological posterior probabilities to the sensitivity ranges.

## Results: query-relative representation

Preserving count and basal-area second moments at the initial state does not
preserve individual juvenile membership or imply that stochastic transitions
commute with projection. The original observed-size audit and enriched-state
propagation remain available, with revised growth and inference separately
identified. Cap experiments retain every declared value; none is chosen using
later validation outcomes. Fine individual origin-size reconstruction remains
an explicit unsupported bridge rather than being filled with invented profiles.

Context experiments change implemented priors, information and approximate
estimation. They are not a single fixed-law optimal Bayes decision problem.
Coarser-context improvements can therefore reflect prior mismatch or
regularization without contradicting the theorem that additional information
cannot worsen optimal risk under a fixed joint law, action space and loss.

## Discussion

The central achievement is a source-qualified observation-to-capacity analysis
whose mathematical and empirical meanings remain distinguishable. Mathematical
corrections are tested at reached implementation branches; unfavorable
calibration is retained; missing support is exposed at the target where it
matters. Higher sampling precision addresses numerical ambiguity, not missing
biological identification. A richer representation can improve a query while
changing its modeled dynamics, and a formal Health definition does not by itself
validate a particular ecological realization.

The adult result is a retrospective independent component test under explicit
left truncation. It is not a publicly issued forecast or full Health validation.
Seedling and entry obstructions delimit what the available observations can
identify. Scenario and semantic thresholds remain declared, with no inferred
ecological prior. Operational forest Health is not established by these component tests:
the whole-plot juvenile target, biological entry and comprehensive ecological
response law remain outside their identified support. Terminal attribution and
full shared-hazard mortality calibration complete the two outstanding
computational obligations; these limitations do not reopen either calculation.

Exact deterministic set refinement under fixed semantics and stochastic
measurement under changing candidate laws answer different questions. This
study addresses the latter. All conclusions attach to a specification,
observational support, result identity and uncertainty category; none derives
empirical authority merely from successful execution.
