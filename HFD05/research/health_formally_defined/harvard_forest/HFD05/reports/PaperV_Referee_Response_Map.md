Three manuscript questions organize the revision: measurement identification and
calibration; decision reliability; query-relative representation. Execution
success is not empirical measurement success. Fixed-semantics deterministic set
refinement belongs to the representation study; changing stochastic generators
and their capacity measures belongs to the present measurement study.
