# Entry identification and missingness tipping

The original6,992 later-only living measured candidate audit is preserved in
full. `entry_identification_matrix.csv` states the necessary observations for
threshold crossing, previously unrecorded eligible stems, new stems of existing
plants, identity/tag changes, frame changes and unresolved entry. An absent earlier
record and the same later tagged record admit both a formerly below-threshold
stem and a formerly eligible missed stem. Their observation maps agree while
biological entry differs. This explicit noninjectivity witness is conceptual,
not manufactured field evidence.

Neither a large later diameter nor a prior-coded parent supplies missing earlier
stem size/emergence dates. E1 plant grouping is derived; missing locations are
not completed. Original low/reference/high membership assignments are diagnostics,
not calibrated class posteriors or biological entry probabilities. Botanical
and tag evidence remain source-provenanced.

The complete B0–B4 R0–R4 propagation retains121,500 scenario/horizon/query cells.
`boundary_conditioned_missingness.csv` derives present/absent flips, signed
margins, original-present near-boundary changes, local maxima and95th absolute
quantiles over every original semantic cell at theta0.75. Default conditional
tipping records preserve individual state/model/scenario/horizon/query identities;
their capacity is the inclusive finite tipping threshold, not a confidence limit.
Measured/imputed basal area and taxon/coverage-proxy contributions link to the
complete controlled-exposure provenance rather than implying observed2020 state.

A separately frozen16-state diagnostic retains every combination of unknown-fate
log hazard shift−0.5/0/+0.5 and future entry multiplier0/0.5/1, D0/D3 and both
queries at allfive thresholds. Unknown is never rewritten as observed death.
Origin unobserved entry remains unchanged; the multiplier applies only to future
candidate-entry intensity. These are diagnostic ranges, not fitted posterior
uncertainty or a global state-space robustness claim. Entry identification,
origin imputation, future Monte Carlo and reference-label uncertainty remain
distinct limitations.

All four alternative families can reverse corrected near-boundary decisions
relative to B0. `family_boundary_summary.csv` retains972 semantic cells per
family/result at theta0.75. Under R4, B1/B2/B3/B4 have24/24/39/42 cells with
present-near reversals; maximum conditional reversal counts are10/7/5/5 of256
units. Across all units, maxima are63/39/30/42. Some local capacity differences
reach1, so family-average summaries cannot establish general robustness. These
paired finite diagnostics include reconstruction/future RNG effects and do not
constitute an ecological causal assignment.
